Hi! It seems like it's your first time using Kawakaze

## Key things you need to know:

- **Kawakaze doesn't host LLMs**, so you need to bring your own provider via OpenRouter or something else. Add it in `Settings` > `LLM Providers`.
- You also need a **persona**, a character representing you in roleplay: `Settings` > `Personas`.
- Browse characters on the **home** tab, hit **play**, and the card is saved to your **library** on this device.
- Characters can be imported from [datacat](https://datacat.run/)'s JSON files (lorebooks are supported).
- **Chats stay on your device.** Signed-in users can move a chat to another device with "Upload to sync" in the chat menu. **Clearing browser storage will result in loss of your data**.
- Kawakaze is a hosted edition of [Ägir](https://github.com/MilesVII/agir): backups and exported cards and chats work in both.
- Want to publish your own characters? Sign in, redeem an invite on your profile and use the editor in your library.
