# Terms of use

Kawakaze is a small, non-commercial community service for sharing roleplay scenario cards. By using it you agree to the following.

## Content

- **Nothing illegal.** Don't publish, upload or post anything that's illegal where the service is hosted or where you are. This includes, without being limited to, sexual content involving minors, real people depicted without consent, doxxing and content that infringes someone else's rights.
- **Only publish what you have the rights to.** If you didn't make it and it isn't shared under a compatible license, don't publish it here.
- **Everything published is shared under the [WTFPL](http://www.wtfpl.net/about/).** By publishing a scenario, a picture, a comment or a profile bio you release it under the WTFPL: anyone can copy, change, fork and redistribute it, here or anywhere else. Forking other people's Kawakaze cards is explicitly allowed.
- Self-promotion is fine: link your other pages, socials or support platforms in your bio and card descriptions.

## The service

- Kawakaze is non-commercial and comes with **no guarantees**: not for uptime, not for keeping your data, not for privacy. Treat everything you upload as public.
- Chats, personas and LLM provider keys stay on your device. They only reach the server when you explicitly upload a chat to sync it to another device; it is deleted from the server once downloaded.
- The server stores what's needed to run accounts: your OAuth provider id, username, profile, sessions (with browser user agent), published content, comments, votes and notifications. Anonymous comments store a salted hash of the IP address for abuse control.
- Admins may remove any content and suspend any account, for any reason, with or without notice.

## Accounts

- Sign in with GitHub or Google. Publishing requires an invite.
- You can delete your account at any time from your profile. Since published content is under the WTFPL, copies others already made are theirs to keep.
